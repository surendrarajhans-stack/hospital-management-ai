MEDSPHERE WITH SAI NURSING HOME

Data Security, Privacy & Regulatory Compliance Brief

MedSphere AI is engineered around bank-grade security infrastructures to guarantee clinical data confidentiality, patient privacy, and absolute defense against unauthorized access or breaches. The architecture is built to comply with India’s national healthcare guidelines and data protection acts.

1. Infrastructure & Storage Security

🔐 Cloud Database Encrypted Storage (AES-256)

All electronic health records (EHR), prescriptions, and billing logs are hosted securely on MongoDB Atlas Cloud Clusters. Data is permanently encrypted at rest using AES-256 algorithms and replicated across secure cloud availability zones to prevent loss.

🛡️ Strict IP Access Whitelisting & Firewalls

The database is protected behind rigid network firewalls. Direct connection access is strictly limited to pre-approved application IP addresses (IP Whitelisting). Any external connection attempt is instantly refused and logged as a security alert.

2. Data Transit Security

🌐 End-to-End SSL/TLS Encryption (TLS 1.3)

All data packets transferred between the user's browser and our server are fully encrypted using TLS 1.3 security protocols. This prevents eavesdropping, packet sniffing, or man-in-the-middle attacks on the hospital's local network or public Wi-Fi.

3. Application Governance

🔑 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Staff members operate strictly within their clearance level boundaries. Receptionists are restricted to demographic entry, nurses to bed grids, pharmacists to drug inventory, and doctors to clinical diagnostics. Super administrative privileges are guarded behind multi-factor protocols.

4. Indian National Health & Privacy Compliance

  • DPDP ACT 2023 Digital Personal Data Protection Act: Fully aligned with India's DPDP Act guidelines. Patient consent rules, role-based boundaries, and immutable logs ensure patient data protection.
  • ABDM COMPLIANT Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (M3): Compliant with ABDM interoperability, allowing secure, authorized health records exchange with linked ABHA IDs.
  • HIPAA ALIGNED Healthcare Standards: Formatted in alignment with HIPAA patient privacy parameters and secure audit trail logging.
