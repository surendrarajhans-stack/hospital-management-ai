MEDSPHERE CLINICAL COPILOT

Standalone Mobile App Launch Roadmap & Monetization Blueprint

A B2B / B2C startup blueprint for packaging MedSphere’s core AI decision-support tools into an independent, lightweight, offline-first mobile application deployable directly on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

1. The Strategic Value Proposition

💡 Frictionless Mobile Rounds Helper

Traditional Hospital Information Systems (HIS) require institutional procurement, months of onboarding, and heavy IT installations. A standalone mobile copilot can be downloaded individually by physicians, residents, and clinical interns on their personal smartphones and used instantly during rounds.

2. Core Feature Matrix

📱 Phase 1: MVP (Offline Drug-Drug Safety & Lab Matrix)

Includes a high-speed search database matching drug combinations of up to 5 medications simultaneously. Works 100% offline to support deep-ward hospital basements where connectivity is unavailable. Features manual input logs for tracking lab reports and highlighting out-of-bound anomalies in red.

📸 Phase 2: Camera AI Report Scanner (OCR)

Integrates phone camera scanning. The doctor takes a photo of a paper diagnostic report, and the app automatically extracts values, highlights clinical alerts, and drafts clinical explanation briefs.

3. Monetization Architecture (Freemium Model)

  • FREE TIER Core Lookups: Unlimited manual drug-drug checks and manual lab anomaly entry tools to drive virality and quick hospital adoption.
  • PREMIUM TIER Camera Scanner & Case AI: Priced at ₹199 to ₹499 monthly. Unlocks camera-based report reading, pediatric dose calculators, and AI clinical summaries.

4. Technology Stack

  • Cross-Platform Codebase: React Native / Flutter (single codebase for Google Play and iOS App Store).
  • Local Storage: SQLite or Realm DB for zero-latency, offline drug monographs and contraindication search tables.
  • AI Model: Google Gemini API integrations for report interpretations.
