MedSphere AI — Doctor Presentation & Demo Playbook

A step-by-step guide to running a successful product demonstration and negotiation meeting with clinical heads and hospital administration.

1. Pre-Meeting Check-list (Tonight's Setup)

  • Clean Database State: Open the application in your browser and click the red "Reset System Data" button in the top header. This ensures the dashboard displays clean, standardized templates with no random test entries.
  • Pre-seeded Doctor Profile: Make sure your local code contains the head doctor's name on DOC-001. (We have already renamed DOC-001 to "Dr. Agrawal Sir" across the entire codebase!)
  • Device Ready: Ensure you have the dashboard loaded on a laptop (or tablet) with an active internet connection.

2. The 10-Minute Demo Flow

Doctors value efficiency and clinical utility. Do not explain technical code—walk them through a single patient's clinical lifecycle:

Step 1: Registrar Check-in (The Intake)

  • Open the Manual Patient Entry Desk (OPD Registrar portal).
  • Register a new mock patient (e.g., Amit Patel, 62 Years, Complaint: Acute chest pain).
  • Click "Generate ABHA ID" to simulate a real-time government digital health ID mapping.
  • The Pitch: "Our intake panel takes less than 60 seconds. It is Indian ABDM compliant, allowing you to instantly generate or link patients' ABHA health cards."

Step 2: Ward Bed Grid (The Nurse Desk)

  • Navigate to the Nurse Console & Ward Manager.
  • Point out the color-coded Interactive Ward Bed Grid (vacant beds are green, occupied beds are red).
  • Click on the bed assigned to your mock patient. Log fresh vitals (Temp, Heart Rate, BP, SpO2).
  • The Pitch: "Your nursing staff gets an interactive map of all active beds (ICU, General Wards, Pediatrics). Logging vitals updates the database in real-time, syncing to the cloud so you can review them from anywhere."

Step 3: Clinical Safety Copilot (Doctor Desk)

  • Go to the Doctor Console (logged in as Dr. Agrawal Sir). Click on the patient from the queue.
  • AI Lab Report Scan: Show the pathology values, click check, and watch the system highlight anomalies (high glucose/creatinine) in red.
  • Drug-Drug Interaction Alert: In the medication field, add Sildenafil, then add Nitroglycerin. The red warning banner will instantly flash.
  • The Pitch: "Our software acts as a clinical copilot. It alerts you to dangerous abnormal lab results and flags contraindicated drug combinations before you sign the digital prescription."

Step 4: Discharging & Payments (The Checkout)

  • Navigate to the Pharmacist & Discharge Billing Desk.
  • Select the patient from the dropdown list. Click "Compute Discharge Bill".
  • Point out the "WhatsApp Bill Receipt" function.
  • The Pitch: "When the patient is discharged, we don't print paper. A secure UPI/Card checkout link and digital GST invoice are sent directly to their WhatsApp. Your clinic gets paid faster, and patients don't stand in long checkout lines."

3. How to Pitch the Commercials (Value Proposition)

No Per-User Licensing Fees:
Standard hospital software charges per doctor or per consultant, which penalizes hospital growth. MedSphere AI uses a Flat Bed-Based Rate. The hospital pays only for their operational beds. They can add unlimited doctors, nurses, and receptionists at zero extra cost.

4. Handling Objections (FAQs)

Objection How to Answer
"Where is our patient data stored?" "All data is encrypted (AES-256) and stored on secure cloud servers using MongoDB Atlas. It is fully compliant with Indian DPDP Act 2023 guidelines."
"Is it hard for nurses or billing staff to learn?" "The software is designed to look like everyday messaging apps. If a staff member knows how to use WhatsApp or a smartphone, they can learn to use MedSphere in less than 15 minutes."
"Can we integrate our existing diagnostic lab software?" "Yes, our IT importer allows you to drag-and-drop standard CSV/Excel roster logs directly, mapping them to the database in seconds."
