INDEPENDENT REFERRAL AGENT AGREEMENT

Technoconsultancy and Services • MedSphere AI

This Independent Referral Agent Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of the date of signing, by and between Technoconsultancy and Services (the "Company") and the undersigned Referral Agent (the "Agent").

1. Nature of Relationship
The Agent acts strictly as an independent contractor. This Agreement does not create any employer-employee, partnership, or joint venture relationship between the Agent and the Company. The Agent is not entitled to any base salary, sick leave, insurance, or other employee benefits.

2. Scope of Services

The Agent shall introduce, market, and refer potential healthcare clinics, private nursing homes, and hospitals ("Prospective Clients") to the Company’s cloud software application (MedSphere AI).

3. Compensation & Commission Structure

  1. Zero Base Pay: The Agent agrees to operate on a 100% commission-only success model.
  2. Commission Share: The Company shall pay the Agent a commission of 25% of the net subscription revenue received and cleared in the Company's bank account from the referred client.
  3. Payment Duration: The commission is payable annually for the **entire active lifetime of the client subscription**, for as long as the referred client continues their subscription payments with the Company (no first-year cap).
  4. Cleared Funds Rule: Commissions are calculated and paid annually (within 30 days of the end of each annual subscription cycle) for subscription payments successfully cleared during the previous year. If a client defaults or stops paying, the Agent’s commission for that client halts immediately.

4. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

The Agent shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all proprietary software, source codes, credentials, pricing strategies, and client data. The Agent shall not copy, reverse-engineer, or share MedSphere AI software materials with any third party.

5. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by providing 15 days' written notice. Upon termination, the Company shall continue to pay the Agent commission only for active clients onboarded prior to the termination date, for the remainder of their active subscription lifetime.
