MEDSPHERE WITH SAI NURSING HOME

B2B SaaS Go-To-Market & Sales Team Hiring Playbook

A strategic blueprint for recruiting, onboarding, and scaling a commission-incentivized field marketing workforce across Indian Tier-1 metro regions to accelerate MedSphere AI clinical software adoption.

1. The Ideal Marketing Executive Persona

🎯 The Medical Representative (MR) Advantage
Instead of hiring expensive software sales executives who demand high fixed retainers, target active Medical Representatives (MRs) or Medical Device Sales Agents. These candidates already possess direct gatekeeper-free access to doctors' chambers, established clinical relationships, and high regional credibility.

2. Recruitment & Compensation Strategy

To scale rapidly while preserving startup capital, employ a performance-linked compensation structure:

Hiring Model Compensation Structure Primary Target Profile
Full-Time Rep ₹20,000 Base Salary + ₹5,000 success bonus per hospital contract (Monthly Target: 3 conversions). Junior MRs or medical sales graduates.
Part-Time / Referral MR ₹0 Base Salary + 25% commission share of the hospital's subscription fee, paid annually for the lifetime of the active subscription. Experienced MRs looking for secondary income streams.
