AI Clinical Safety: Configured Drug-Drug Interaction Checklist

The following table details the 10 critical drug-drug interaction pairings configured within the MedSphere AI Clinical Decision Support (CDS) engine. The system automatically cross-analyzes added medications in real-time to alert the consulting practitioner prior to prescription dispatch.

Prescribed Pairing Clinical Impact Detailed Contraindication & Safety Action
Sildenafil + Nitroglycerin Severe Hypotension Profound, life-threatening systemic vasodilation. Dual use is strictly contraindicated.
Spironolactone + Lisinopril Hyperkalemia Risk of severe hyperkalemia. Potentially fatal arrhythmias. Close electrolyte monitoring required.
Aspirin + Warfarin Bleeding Risk Synergistic antiplatelet and anticoagulant effects. Significantly elevates major bleeding and GI hemorrhage risk.
Digoxin + Clarithromycin Digoxin Toxicity Clarithromycin inhibits P-gp, doubling Digoxin exposure. High risk of severe nausea, visual halos, and heart block.

Clopidogrel + Omeprazole Efficacy Loss Omeprazole inhibits CYP2C19, reducing Clopidogrel activation and elevating thrombosis risk. Use Pantoprazole instead.
Ibuprofen + Aspirin Efficacy Loss Ibuprofen binds competitively to COX-1, blocking the permanent cardioprotective antiplatelet effect of low-dose Aspirin.
Simvastatin + Amlodipine Myopathy Risk Amlodipine increases Simvastatin levels. Limit Simvastatin to a maximum of 20mg daily under concurrent use.
Amiodarone + Warfarin Bleeding Risk Amiodarone inhibits CYP2C9, slowing Warfarin clearance and raising bleeding risks. Reduce Warfarin dose by 30-50%.
