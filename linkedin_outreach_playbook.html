A B2B social-selling guide to utilizing LinkedIn Premium (InMail, Advanced Search) to recruit independent Medical Representatives (MRs) and pitch clinical operating systems directly to hospital owners.

💡 Why LinkedIn Premium is a Game-Changer
1. Direct InMails: Bypasses connection requests, placing your pitch directly in their primary inbox.
2. Search Filters: Targets candidates by current job title, company tier, and exact metro geography (Delhi, Bangalore, Bhubaneswar).

1. Recruiting Sales Partners (Active MRs)

Use these search terms to find candidates who already visit doctors daily:

  • Search Keywords: "Medical Representative" OR "Pharma Sales" OR "Medical Device Sales"
  • Location Filters: Bhubaneswar, Delhi NCR, or Bangalore

Subject: Partnership opportunity: medical sales in [City]

We are launching MedSphere AI, an ABDM-compliant AI Hospital Operating System featuring real-time drug interaction checks and lab anomaly scanners. Since you already have relationships with clinic owners, we'd like to partner. We offer a 25% recurring annual commission per clinic onboarded (no first-year cap, earning potential ₹3-5L/yr, T&C apply) with zero base pay. Let me know if you are open to a 5-minute call. Regards, Surendra Rajhans."

2. Pitching Direct Clients (Private Clinic / Hospital Owners)

  • Search Keywords: "Clinic Owner" OR "Managing Director Hospital" OR "Chief Medical Officer"
  • Location Filters: Bhubaneswar, Delhi NCR, or Bangalore
