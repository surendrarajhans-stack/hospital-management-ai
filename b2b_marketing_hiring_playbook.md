# B2B SaaS Sales Team Hiring Playbook

A strategic guide to recruiting, onboarding, and incentivizing marketing/sales executives to scale *MedSphere AI* across Indian Tier-1 metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata).

---

## 🎯 1. The Ideal Candidate Persona

Instead of hiring standard corporate software sales reps who demand high fixed packages, your highest ROI will come from recruiting active **Medical Representatives (MRs)** or **Medical Device Distributors**.

### Why Medical Representatives (MRs)?
* **Existing Gatekeeper Access:** They already know how to bypass receptionist desks and directly access doctors' chambers.
* **Built-in Trust:** They have nurtured relationships with private clinic and nursing home owners over years of selling pharmaceuticals.
* **Low Initial Fixed Cost:** Many MRs are willing to work on a freelance referral or a hybrid commission structure (base + success bonus).

---

## 💼 2. Compensation & Incentive Models

To keep your startup’s cash burn low, implement a **Base + High Commission** structure:

| Model Type | Details | Recommended Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Full-Time Rep** | ₹20,000 Base Salary + ₹5,000 bonus per hospital onboarded (minimum 3 conversions/mo target). | Young sales graduates or junior MRs looking to switch to healthcare tech. |
| **Part-Time / Referral MR** | ₹0 Base Salary + 25% recurring share of the hospital's subscription fee for the first 12 months. | Experienced pharmaceutical reps with established clinic networks. |

Use this message template to reach out to potential candidates on WhatsApp or LinkedIn:

> *"Hi [Name], I saw your profile and background in medical sales. We are launching an AI-powered, ABDM-compliant Hospital Operating System (MedSphere AI) that helps private clinics and nursing homes digitize clinical operations.
>
> What makes our software unique and highly sellable is our built-in **AI Clinical Safety Suite**:
> 1. **AI Drug-Drug Interaction Checker:** Flags contraindicated medications in real-time to prevent prescribing errors.
> 2. **AI Lab Anomaly Detector:** Scans pathology reports to flag panic lab values (like cardiac troponin spikes).
> 3. **AI Emergency Triage Router:** Automatically calculates patient acuity scores (ESI 1-5) and routes beds.
> 4. **AI Patient Agent:** A Gemini-powered clinical copilot for symptom checking and self-service.
>
> We are expanding our field team in [City] and looking for sales partners who already have strong relationships with doctors. It offers a 100% success-based 25% recurring monthly commission per hospital sign-up. Let me know if you are open to a quick call to discuss details. Regards, Surendra Rajhans."*

## 🚀 4. The 30-Day Launch Plan

1. **Days 1–10 (The Pilot):** Use your first customer (e.g. *Sai Nursing Home*) to create a 3-page regional case study showing cost savings and ABDM compliance.
2. **Days 11–20 (Hiring):** Hire exactly **two** pilot representatives (one in Delhi NCR, one in Bangalore).
3. **Days 21–30 (Onboarding):** Equip them with the booklet (`medsphere_booklet.html`) and security sheet, and have them visit 5 clinics a day.
