MedSphere AI Hospital OS | Regulatory & Terms Disclosure

1. Clinical Decision Support EULA & Medical Disclaimer

MedSphere AI is an administrative hospital operating system and clinical decision-support assistant. All AI-generated symptom analysis, diagnostic laboratory anomaly flags, and triage scores are provided strictly to assist healthcare staff. MedSphere AI does not issue independent medical diagnoses. Final clinical diagnosis, prescription verification, and treatment authorization remain the sole responsibility of the licensed treating medical practitioner.

2. Data Privacy & DPDP Act 2023 / HIPAA Encryption Standards

Patient electronic health records (EHR), registration data, and billing transactions processed through MedSphere AI are encrypted in transit and at rest using 256-bit SSL/TLS protocol. In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and HIPAA guidelines, patient records are protected against unauthorized access, with role-based access control (RBAC) and immutable audit trails logged for all system interactions.

3. ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) & ABHA ID Linkage

MedSphere AI supports 14-digit ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) Health ID generation, validation, and PM-JAY scheme cashless claims processing. Health records generated under this license adhere to ABDM M3 interoperability specifications for seamless electronic health record exchange across accredited hospital nodes.
